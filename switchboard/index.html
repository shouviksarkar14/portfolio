    Watchlist

    Where roles come from. LinkedIn job-alert emails in your Gmail, hirist and other portal digests, RapidAPI Job Search (LinkedIn, Wellfound, YC; once connected), and searches of Ashby, Greenhouse and Lever boards. Each sync keeps roles posted in the last 24h. Watchlist companies get +15 fit and rank first.

    How fit is scored. Fit starts at 50. Product company, consumer, platform or B2B SaaS work, and an India or Remote location each add points. Consulting, forward-deployed, bank Product Owner and 8+ year roles subtract points.

    Rounds

    How this list is built

    Sources. Each sync reads interview invites, reschedules, assessment links and calendar files in your Gmail and adds or updates a round on the matching application. Every card links back to the email it came from. You can also add or fix a round from an application's panel.

    What a card shows. Date and time in IST, the meeting link, who you meet, the recruiter to contact, the job post, and prep notes taken from the invite.

    After a round. Mark it done and log how it went. The application's status stays yours to move.

    Rules

    Applied at each sync

    How it works

    1. Each sync takes new roles with a direct Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever or Workable link and prepares an application: the CV that fits the role, your profile fields and draft answers.
    2. Roles at or above your fit bar with no written questions are submitted at the next sync, up to your daily limit.
    3. Roles with written questions wait here. Edit the answers and approve, and the next sync submits them.
    4. Portals that need a login (LinkedIn, Workday, Naukri, company portals) stay manual. The card links the post and gives you the answers to paste.
    5. Every submission is logged in Pipeline as Applied, with the CV used.

    POC finder

    Runs at the next sync
    Pick an application or role. At the next sync, Claude checks the recruiter named on the posting, then the company's careers and team pages and public profiles, and adds up to 3 likely contacts. Each contact carries a confidence tag and a draft note. Nothing is sent for you.
